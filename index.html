Google Block Breaker : The Addictive Brick Breaking Game

Welcome to Google Block Breaker, the most exciting brick breaking game that challenges your reflexes and strategic thinking! Whether you call it Block Breaker or Block Breaker Google, this game delivers endless fun as you smash colorful blocks and aim for the highest score.

How to Play Google Block Breaker

  1. Use your mouse or touchpad to move the paddle left and right
  2. Bounce the ball to break all the blocks at the top of the screen
  3. Don't let the ball fall past your paddle you all lose a life!
  4. Collect power ups to gain special abilities like multi,ball or paddle expansion
  5. Clear all blocks to advance to the next challenging level
