How to Play Low's Adventures 2

  • Move: Use the Arrow Keys or WASD to move Low left, right, and jump.
  • Avoid enemies: Jump over or onto enemies to defeat them.
  • Collect coins: Grab coins scattered throughout the levels for bonus points.
  • Reach the flag: Complete each level by reaching the end flag.
  • Watch out: Don’t fall into pits or touch dangerous objects, or you’ll lose a life.
